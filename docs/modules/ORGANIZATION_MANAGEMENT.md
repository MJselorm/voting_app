# ORGANIZATION_MANAGEMENT.md — Tenant Governance & Administration

## 1. Overview

The **Organization Management** module empowers Platform Super Admins and Organization Admins to manage institutional lifecycles, configuration settings, departmental hierarchies, and staff delegations.

---

## 2. Platform Super Admin Operations

The Platform Super Admin governs the ecosystem through the Platform Admin Console:
- **Organization Onboarding**:
  - Review submitted organization requests.
  - Approve or decline tenant creation.
  - Designate the initial **Organization Admin**.
- **Tenant State Supervision**:
  - Suspend organizations for non-compliance, non-payment, or administrative investigation.
  - Reactivate or archive decommissioned organizations.
- **Global Audit & Monitoring**:
  - Cross-tenant activity feeds, active election monitoring, and platform-wide user statistics.

---

## 3. Organization Admin Operations

Once appointed, the Organization Admin manages localized institutional operations:
- **Branding & Institutional Profile**:
  - Name, slug, institutional logo, primary colors, official website, and contact email.
- **Departments & Membership Groups**:
  - Definition of academic faculties, departments, sports clubs, or corporate branches.
  - Configurable custom profile fields (e.g. "Year of Admission", "Payroll Code").
- **Verification Policies**:
  - Allowed email domain patterns (e.g. `@st.ug.edu.gh`).
  - Strict vs. relaxed name-matching thresholds.
- **Staff Role Delegations**:
  - Appoint **Election Officials** to oversee specific elections.
  - Appoint **Election Super Admins** to certify results.
  - Grant **Poll Creator** privileges to faculty reps, club leaders, or marketing staff.
